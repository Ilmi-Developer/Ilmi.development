Bring in your students

{% if students %}

You have {{ students }} student{{ "s" if students != 1 }} on Ilmi already. Uploading a newer list updates them and never adds anyone twice.

{% endif %}

Upload your student list as a CSV or an Excel (.xlsx) file. Each row is one student; each student goes into their teacher’s classroom, and classrooms that don’t exist yet are made for you. You see a preview of every change before anything is saved.

  1. Get your list ready. One student on each row, with the column names in the first row. Only the student’s name and their Qur’an teacher or class are needed; Student ID, gender, year group{{ ", house" if cfg.houses_on }} and date of birth are taken too if they are there. Your own column names are fine: the preview lets you match them. Download the template
  2. Upload it on Classes. The button below opens the uploader. Choose the file, check the preview, and confirm.
  3. Invite your staff. On Manage users, invite each teacher and give them their classrooms.
