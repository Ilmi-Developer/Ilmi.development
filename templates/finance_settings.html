The madrasah

Which should I choose?

Charity reports (the Statement of Financial Activities and receipts and payments) appear on Reports only for a registered charity, a CIO or a charitable company. Not every madrasah is a charity.

Smaller charities in England and Wales can often prepare receipts and payments accounts; larger ones, and charitable companies, use accruals. Your accountant or independent examiner can say which applies. Ilmi keeps full records either way.

Terms follow the financial year: with a September start, Autumn is September to December, Spring January to March and Summer April to August.
