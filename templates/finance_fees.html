How this works

Bill a fee to one student, or the same fee to everyone in a class. A fee can be split into monthly instalments, each with its own due date, and can have a discount taken off.

Open a fee to record a payment (part payments are fine), take more off as a discount or waiver, or pay money back. Nothing is ever deleted: a mistake is corrected with an opposite entry, and both stay in the fee’s history.

Students come from the classes. Fees are grouped into families by the Family ID on each student’s profile.
