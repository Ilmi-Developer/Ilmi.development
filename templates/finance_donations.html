Donations & funds

Gifts, fundraising and grants, and what each fund holds.

How this works

A restricted fund holds money given for one purpose only, such as a building appeal or a Zakat fund. It can only be spent on that purpose. Everything else goes in the General fund, which can be spent on anything.

When you record spending on Income & expenses, choose the fund it comes from, so each fund’s balance stays right.
