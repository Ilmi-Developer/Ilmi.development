How these are worked out

Income and spending count fees when they are billed and bills when they are due, whether or not the money has moved yet. The cash flow report shows only money that actually moved.

Fees collected is how much of the fees billed in this period has been paid so far, after discounts. Needs attention counts every fee and bill still open, whatever the period.

Funds show what each fund holds today. Equipment bought from a fund still counts in it, as something the madrasah owns.
