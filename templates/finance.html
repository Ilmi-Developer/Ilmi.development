How these are worked out

Income and spending count fees when they are billed and bills when they are due, whether or not the money has moved yet. The cash flow report shows only money that actually moved.

Fees collected is how much of the fees billed in this period has been paid so far, after discounts.

Cash and bank is what the books say you hold today. Check it against your bank statement on Settings so you know it’s right.
