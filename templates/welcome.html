What it does

Four things, done well.

Everything a madrasah keeps track of, in one calm record.

  • {% include "art/i-quran.svg" %}01

    Qur’an tracking

    Score each lesson on the day-by-day sheet, and record Qaidah levels and book passes.

  • {% include "art/i-memo.svg" %}02

    Memorisation

    Memo levels, weekly targets and level tests, followed pupil by pupil.

  • {% include "art/i-houses.svg" %}03

    Houses and rewards

    Makkah, Madinah and Al-Aqsa: tokens, house points and the race on the hall TV.

  • {% include "art/i-progress.svg" %}04

    Progress at a glance

    Rankings, pass history and pupils by level, so you can see growth and who needs help.

The idea

Lessons, levels, passes and points used to live in notebooks, spreadsheets and memory. Ilmi keeps them together, so every teacher can see each pupil’s next step, and no effort goes unnoticed.

Every class, every pupil, in one place.

A clear, calm view of your classes and each pupil’s learning, so you can spend more time teaching and less time organising.

  1. Score every lessonThe day’s Qur’an lessons, scored on one sheet.
  2. Follow memo targetsSee who met their target and who is still to test.
  3. Run the house racePoints add up live, on the overview and the hall TV.
