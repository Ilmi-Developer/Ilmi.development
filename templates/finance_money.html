Income & expenses

Money that isn’t a student fee: donations, grants and other income, and everything the madrasah spends.

How this works

Record money in when it arrives. Tuition fees go on Student fees instead, so they count against what each family owes.

Record money out as paid now, or as a bill not paid yet. A bill counts as spending straight away and shows under Unpaid bills until you pay it. Equipment you buy is kept as something the madrasah owns, not as spending.

Choose a restricted fund when the money can only be used for one purpose, such as a building appeal.
