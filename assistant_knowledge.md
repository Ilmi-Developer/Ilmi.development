# Ilmi assistant knowledge base

This file is what Ilmi's assistant (Ask Ilmi) answers from. It is loaded into the assistant's instructions on every question,
so keep it accurate and plain. Anything marked [CHECK] was drafted from the code and should be confirmed by the
school; anything in [SQUARE BRACKETS AND CAPITALS] is a placeholder for the school to fill in. Remove the marks once
checked.

## A. Using the site

The website is Ilmi (علمي, "my knowledge"), the madrasah manager. Everyone signs in with their own email and password. What you see depends on
your role:

- **Teachers** see only the classes an administrator has given them.
- **HeadTeachers** see every class, and also have Record, House points, Rankings, Presenter and FAQs. They have none
  of the Administration pages.
- **Administrators** see everything, including the Administration pages (Students, Manage users, Activity, Class
  history, Counted coins, Spreadsheet, Import, FAQs, Assistant requests and Presenter for the TV screens).

The menu is on the left on a computer. On a phone it is the bar at the bottom of the screen (the dock); **More**
opens the full menu.

### Signing in and out

1. Open the site. Signed out, you land on the start page; press **Sign in** at the top right.
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tick **Stay signed in for 30 days** if this is your own device. Leave it unticked on a shared computer.
4. Press **Sign in**.

- Administrators are then emailed a 6-digit code. Type it on the **Check your email** page. If no email arrives
  after a minute, check your spam folder or sign in again for a new code.
- If you didn't tick "Stay signed in", closing the browser signs you out. You are also signed out 20 minutes after
  the dashboard was last open, and after 12 hours at most.
- To sign out, press **Sign out** under your name at the bottom of the menu.
- Five wrong passwords in 15 minutes stops sign-in for that email for a while. Wait, then try again, or use
  **Forgotten your password?**

### Forgotten password

1. On the **Sign in** page, press **Forgotten your password?**
2. Type the email you sign in with and press **Email me a reset link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link within 1 hour. Choose a new password (at least 12 characters) and type it twice.
4. Using the link signs you out on your other devices.

If no email comes, check your spam folder. If reset emails aren't set up, the page says so: ask an administrator to
send you a reset link from Manage users. Your old password keeps working until you use the link.

### First sign-in (invite)

An administrator invites you from Manage users. You get a link, usually by email, to choose your password. Invite
links work once and expire after 72 hours; ask an administrator for a new one if yours has expired.

### Your account (Account page)

Open it with **Account** under your name at the bottom of the menu.

- **Change your password**: type your **Current password**, then the **New password** twice, and press **Change
  password**. You stay signed in on this device; other devices are signed out.
- **Profile picture**: press **Upload**, choose a photo, drag and zoom it under **Adjust your picture**, then press
  **Save picture**. **Remove picture** takes it away, and your first initial shows instead.
- **Dark mode**: turn it on for a darker screen in a dim room. The sign-in page always stays light.
- **Other devices**: **Sign out on other devices** signs you out everywhere except here.
- **Tutorial**: **Retake the tutorial** runs the short step-by-step tour of the dashboard again.

### Finding your class

1. Press **Classes** in the menu. Teachers see **Your classes**; HeadTeachers and administrators see every class.
2. Press a class's card to open it. On the overview, a teacher can also press **Open tracker** to go straight to
   their class.
3. Inside a class, the tabs along the top are **Quran**, **Memo**, **Progress** and **Settings**.
4. To change class, use **Switch class** next to the class name, or go back to **Classes**.

Classes are shown with the year, class letter and teacher's title in full. For example, the class stored as
"Yr3-A-QM-Br Tariq" shows as "Yr3A Ustadh Tariq".

If you see "No classrooms yet", an administrator hasn't added you to your classes yet. Ask them to tick your classes
on Manage users.

### The overview

**Overview** is the first page. Teachers see a **Class overview**; HeadTeachers and administrators see the **School
overview**. It shows who needs attention (for example students with no pass in 3 or more weeks), this term's
activity, the houses and top classes, and where students are in the book. Press a section's title to see its full
list. [CHECK: confirm the sections a teacher's overview shows]

### Recording Quran scores (Quran tab)

1. Open your class and choose the **Quran** tab.
2. The highlighted column is the class day you are recording. Use the arrows either side of the date to move to the
   previous or next class day. **Latest class day** jumps back to the most recent one.
3. Tap a student's box and choose a score from 0 to 5. On a computer you can also select the box and type 0 to 5.
4. Tap the same score again to clear it.
5. To mark a student absent, choose **Absent** (the dash) in the same picker.

- Changes save straight away; there is no Save button.
- A blank box means no score was recorded. That is different from 0.
- Absent days count as recorded but are left out of every average.
- Class days are Saturdays and Sundays. The calendar button (**Go to a class day**) goes to any day.
- **Last 6 weeks** and **All** choose how far back the days go.
- The **Full sheet** button shows every day at once, on a computer only.
- A score with a gold border is a level test score from the Record page. Only an admin can change it.

### Merits, warnings and statistics (the menu on a student's name)

On the Quran or Memo tab, press a student's name to open their menu.

- **Merits**: **+** gives a merit and **−** takes one back.
- **Warning**: choose **Warning 1**, **Warning 2** or **Miss break**. Press the chosen one again to remove it.
- **Current target** shows where the student is in the syllabus.
- **Statistics** opens the student's statistics: their numbers, charts of their Quran scores and memorisation, and
  where they are in the books.

To clear every warning in the class at once, see the Settings tab below.

### Star of the week

1. On the **Quran** tab, press the star button (**Star of the week**).
2. Choose the student for this week.

It can't be chosen on a Saturday; the button says so. [CHECK: confirm the steps after pressing the star]

### Printing reports for parents

1. On the **Quran** tab, press the printer button (**Print reports**).
2. Choose the students and the period, then print. Each student gets one page: their numbers, their progress, and
   space for the teacher's comments. [CHECK: confirm the choices shown before printing]

### The Memo tab (Tracker and Students)

The Memo tab has two views, **Tracker** and **Students**, switched at the top right.

**Tracker**: marking each class day.

1. The highlighted column is the class day. Use the arrows to change day.
2. Tap a student's box and choose:
   - **+** if they had a memorisation test,
   - the **book** if they went down for a level test,
   - **–** if they were absent.
3. Tap the same mark again to clear it. On a computer: select a box and type + (or T), B (or L), or – (or A).

Absences are shared with the Quran tab: a student marked absent on one shows as absent on the other.

The Tracker marks that a test happened. **The pass itself is recorded on the Record page** by whoever tests the
student (an administrator or HeadTeacher), or added as a past pass on the Progress tab (below). [CHECK]

**Students**: each student's position and passes. Students who should pass soon (three weeks or more since their last
pass) come first. Press a student for their profile, where **Details** shows their details and **History** shows their
full record of passes and fails. **Rename** in Details changes the student's name. [CHECK]

### Progress tab

- **Levels** shows **Position in the book** (each student's place) and **Students by level**.
- **Pass history** shows **Passes per week** (the last 8 weeks, Monday to Sunday) and the list of passes and fails,
  **This month** or **All**.

**Adding a pass that was missed (a past pass):**

1. Open the class, choose **Progress**, then **Pass history**.
2. Press **Add a past pass**.
3. Choose the **Student**, the **Date** and the **Target passed**.
4. Press **Add past pass**.

The date can't be in the future or before the class's first class day. If the target passed is the student's current
target or later, their target moves on to the next one.

**Removing a pass or fail added by mistake:**

1. In **Pass history**, find the pass or fail (press **All** if it's older than this month).
2. Press **Remove** next to it and confirm.

Removing a pass takes one off the student's pass count. It doesn't change their current target.

### Settings tab

- **Rename** changes the class's name. [CHECK: teachers can rename their own class; confirm the school wants this]
- **First class day**: the Quran and Memo sheets start from this day.
- **Clear warnings** removes every student's warning in this class.
- **Reset all scores** sets every student's passes and merits back to zero. Fails, Quran scores and house tokens are
  kept. This can't be undone from the class page, so ask an administrator first. An administrator can restore an
  earlier version from Class history. [CHECK]
- Only administrators can add or remove students, or delete a class. A teacher sees "An administrator adds students
  to your class."

### Presenter (showing your class on the big screen)

1. Open your class and press **Presenter**. [CHECK: confirm where the Presenter button is for teachers]
2. A new tab opens with your class's month on the big screen: podiums for the best Quran average, Qaidah levels,
   house points, the house race and merits; memorisation and book passes; and the stars of the week.
3. Move the mouse to show **Full screen** and **Close**. Close (or Escape) goes back.

Each press of Presenter makes a new link that works only in the tab that opened it. Press Presenter again for a new
one.

### Help or Requests (teachers)

At the bottom of a teacher's overview there is a **Help or Requests** button ("Need a hand?").

1. Press **Help or Requests**.
2. Choose **Help** (something isn't working, or you're not sure how to do it) or **Request** (something new, like a
   change to the dashboard or a resource for your class).
3. Type your message and send it.

The reply appears in the same pop-up, and a red dot on the button shows a reply you haven't opened. The assistant can
also send a request to the administrators for you (see section C).

### House points

Teachers hand students physical house coins. Students put them in the box at the office, and an administrator counts
them and adds each house's total on Counted coins. Coins aren't given on the dashboard. The three houses are
**Makkah**, **Al-Aqsa** and **Madinah**. Teachers can see the whole school's house totals.

| Token | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Green | 5 |
| Blue | 20 |
| Red (book completion) | 50 |

### Record page (HeadTeachers and administrators)

The Record page records level tests and book completions when a student is tested.

1. Press **Record** in the menu.
2. Choose **Quran** (a Qaidah or Tajweed level), **Memo** (a memorisation target) or a book.
3. Choose the student, the level or target, and the score out of 5.
4. Press **Passed** or **Not passed**.

A Memo pass logs a pass and moves the student past that target. A Quran pass sets their Qaidah level. A Memo target can
be chosen by level and target, or by item number and Surah. **Set as current target, no test** sets a student's target
without a test. Book completions are recorded here too, as a pass. [CHECK]

### Ask Ilmi (this chat)

Open **Ask Ilmi** in the menu (on a phone, under **More**). Type a question and press Enter or the arrow. Shift+Enter
starts a new line.

- Typing / at the start of the box shows shortcuts, such as /how, /syllabus, /islam and /request.
- While it works, "Thinking…" shows. Afterwards "Thought for …" can be pressed to see what it did.
- The square button stops an answer that's taking too long. **New chat** starts again.

- Teachers and HeadTeachers: the assistant answers questions about the site, the syllabus and Islam, and can send a
  request to the administrators. It can't see or change any student records.
- Administrators: it can also look up and change student records. Every change is shown first and only happens when
  the administrator presses **Confirm**. Changes can be undone from Class history.
- The conversation is forgotten when you close or reload the tab.
- Each person can ask 30 questions an hour (administrators 60). Teachers and HeadTeachers also have a daily
  allowance; when it's used up, Ask Ilmi says so, and it resets at midnight.

## B. The syllabus

### The book: L1 to L10

Students first work through the memorisation book, levels L1 to L10. Each level has a number of targets:

| Level | Targets |
| --- | --- |
| L1 | 9 |
| L2 | 9 |
| L3 | 9 |
| L4 | 9 |
| L5 | 10 |
| L6 | 10 |
| L7 | 10 |
| L8 | 12 |
| L9 | 15 |
| L10 | 14 |

That is 107 targets. Targets are written "L3 T5" (level 3, target 5). The book ends at **L10 T14**.

### After the book: the Juz items

After L10 T14, students memorise the Quran one juz at a time, starting at **Juz 30** and working back towards **Juz 1**.
The items are numbered:

- **198 An-Naba** is the start of Juz 30. Juz 30 runs to **234 An-Nas**.
- **235 Al-Mulk** starts Juz 29, and so on.
- **332 Al-Baqarah** is the last item (Juz 1). After that the student has completed the Quran.
- A surah that spans two juz (for example Adh-Dhariyat) has an item in each.

**A higher item number means further progress, even though the juz number gets lower.** For example, a student on
205 Al-Buruj is further on than a student on L10 T14, and a student on 240 is further on than one on 205.

### Passes, fails and targets

- A student's **current target** is the next thing they need to pass.
- A **memorisation pass** means the student passed a target. It is logged with its date, and the student moves on to
  the next target.
- **Each pass record is one separate pass.** A student who passed several times has several passes, and every one
  counts, even if two are for the same target.
- A **fail** ("Not passed") is recorded with its date but doesn't change the target or the pass count.
- A student with no passes, no fails and still on the first target has "no data yet".
- "No pass in 3+ weeks" marks a student whose last pass was three or more weeks ago.

### Level tests

At the end of a level a student is sent to the office for a level test, recorded on the Record page:

- **Quran level tests** are for the Qaidah and Tajweed books. Levels 1 to 13 are the **Qaidah Book** and levels 14 to
  20 the **Tajweed Book**. Passing sets the student's Qaidah level, for example "Qaidah Level 4" or "Tajweed Level 15".
- **Memo tests** are for a memorisation target. Passing logs a memorisation pass.
- Each test has a score out of 5, Passed or Not passed, and optionally focus areas to work on (such as Pronunciation,
  Harakah, Madd or Ghunnah) and a note from the tester.
- The score also appears on that day's Quran tab, with a gold border.

### Book completions

When a student finishes a book, it is recorded as a book pass on the Record page. The six books are: **Qā'idah**,
**Qā'idah Part 1**, **Qā'idah Part 2**, **Rules of Tajwīd**, **Memorisation Book 1** and **Memorisation Book 2**. Book
completions are counted separately from memorisation passes. They give no house points on the dashboard.

### Quran scores

Each class day, every student's Quran reading is scored from 0 to 5 on the Quran tab. Absent days count as recorded
but are left out of averages. A student's Quran average is shown to one decimal place.

### Madrasah year groups and classes

A class name such as "Yr3-A-QM-Br Ahmed" means Year 3, class A, Quran and Memorisation, taught by Br Ahmed. The madrasah
year group comes from the class name. A student's school year group (at their primary or secondary school) is kept
separately. Years 2 to 6 (and Year 1 or Reception) count as primary, and Years 7 and above as secondary.

## C. Requests and contacts

The assistant can submit a request for you: a new feature, a change, or a problem report. It writes a one or two
sentence summary, shows it to you, and sends it only after you confirm. Administrators see requests on **Assistant
requests** and mark them done or declined. You can ask the assistant for the status of your own requests. Sending a
request doesn't mean it will be approved.
